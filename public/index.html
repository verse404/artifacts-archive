METHOD 01 · SPATIAL LATTICE

Point-in-Polygon Homeland Grids

Naive maps stack hundreds of markers onto a single capital-city pin. Using Natural Earth 110m vector boundaries, each country’s interior is sampled on an adaptive latitude-longitude lattice (with a golden-angle spiral fallback for small islands) so all 330 Chinese or 293 Egyptian works form a legible national mosaic.

METHOD 02 · GREAT-CIRCLE KINEMATICS

Chronological Gathering & One-Click Repatriation

Every artifact holds dual spherical unit vectors: its homeland grid coordinate and Bloomsbury, London (51.52°N, 0.13°W). Acquisition flights stagger departures between 1753 and 2013; clicking Return Home launches all 4,948 cards simultaneously along cubic-eased parabolic great-circle arcs back to their 99 origins.

METHOD 03 · 64-PLATE HD ATLAS & DIRECT BM LINKS

Verified Provenance & 60 FPS Projection

All 4,948 front-hemisphere cards are backface-culled, painter-sorted by depth, and blitted from a 64-plate high-resolution British Museum sprite atlas—paired with direct links to official British Museum object records and illustrated country catalogues.
